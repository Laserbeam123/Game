Bloons TD Ops - BTD6 art converter
==================================
This folder turns pictures from YOUR OWN Bloons TD 6 install into a private Arma 3 add-on
(<Arma 3>/@btdops/addons/btdops_art.pbo) on your PC. It only reads BTD6's files; it never starts, changes or patches
BTD6, and nothing it makes is uploaded or shared. Bloons TD 6 (c) Ninja Kiwi.

Converter code (btd6art.py, armafmt.py): Bloons TD Ops contributors, MIT licence.
PAA and PBO layouts follow HEMTT (github.com/BrettMayson/HEMTT, GPL-2.0); no HEMTT code is included.
